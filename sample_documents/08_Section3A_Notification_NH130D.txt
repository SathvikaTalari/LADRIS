SECTION 6: OBJECTION PROCEDURE (Section 3B)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Any person interested in any land mentioned in this notification may, within
21 days from the date of publication of this notification, object to:
  (a) the acquisition of the land or any land in the locality;
  (b) the area and suitability of the land; or
  (c) the justification offered for the public purpose.

Objections must be filed in writing with the CALA at the address above.
